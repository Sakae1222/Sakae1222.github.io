## 0730 It' been a year 🎉

It's been a year since I started writing a work log on this blog. Before I started writing here, I felt my shit job was kind of a waste of my life, but after keeping writing, I felt it was not as boring as I thought.

I recorded what I did every day, even when I just slacked off. I reviewed every month here before I made another main blog. I learned a lot of things about web development, but it's still not enough...

This blog changed my life. It helped me keep a balance between my core work time and my low-productivity work time. And I think it changed my opinion on working.

I will keep writing here.
